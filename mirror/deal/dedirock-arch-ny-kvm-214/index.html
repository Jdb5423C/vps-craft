新手选机避坑、回国线路辨析与测速原理解读

CN2 GIA 是中国电信顶级优化网，双程走 59.43 骨干，晚高峰抗拥堵极强、极低延迟不丢包；AS9929 是联通高端 A 网优化线路，负载低、延迟稳定，适合高端多网骨干；AS4837(169 优化) 是联通中端直连网，价格低、带宽大，是 RackNerd/ColoCrossing 的高性价比之选；常规 BGP 则是国际普通线路，适合对延迟不敏感的业务。

CN2 GIA (AS4809) is Telecom’s elite transit path with extremely robust peak-hour performance and zero packet loss. AS9929 (CU Premium) is Unicom’s clean, low-crowd enterprise line. AS4837 (CU Standard) is a budget-optimized transit with great day-time rates. Standard BGP is the regular international route for latency-insensitive workloads.

若用于学习 Linux、托管 Python/Node 脚本、Docker 或个人博客，推荐 RackNerd(年付 $10~$18) 或 ColoCrossing，机器稳定、流量大；若对延迟、游戏加速有顶级需求，首选 BandwagonHost(搬瓦工) 的 CN2 GIA 高端主机。

For lightweight apps, cron jobs, blogs or sandboxed Docker, RackNerd ($10–$18/yr) and ColoCrossing are unmatched for uptime and value. For premium latency (game servers, commercial portals), a BandwagonHost CN2 GIA VPS is strongly recommended.
